Stuffed Grape Leaves

A vegan Mediterranean favorite with herbed rice, pine nuts & currants.

Prep Time: 30 min
Cook Time: 45 min
Servings: 6–8 people

About This Dish

Known as "Zeytinyağlı Yaprak Sarma" in Turkish, these stuffed grape leaves are a staple in Mediterranean cuisine. The recipe is traditionally vegan and served cold with lemon wedges. They are often prepared for gatherings and celebrations, showcasing the delicate balance of herbs, spices, and textures unique to Turkish cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 jar grape leaves in brine (40–50 leaves)
  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts
  • 2 tbsp currants (or raisins)
  • 1/4 cup olive oil (plus more for drizzling)
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp each chopped dill, mint, parsley

Instructions

  1. Soak and rinse grape leaves. Drain well.
  2. Sauté onions in olive oil. Add pine nuts, rice, and seasonings. Simmer with water until absorbed.
  3. Let mixture cool, then stir in herbs. Roll into grape leaves tightly.
  4. Layer rolls in a pot, drizzle with lemon juice and olive oil, add water to cover, and place a plate on top.
  5. Simmer 40–45 min on low. Chill and serve with lemon wedges.
